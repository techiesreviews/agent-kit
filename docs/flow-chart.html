Around the road, not on it

Board check

Every 2 hours: reads the board, restarts stalled work, opens threads for Lex.

Weekly sweep

The week’s numbers, and the corrections Lex gave more than once.

Kit update

Safety rails

No helper without a brief, no direct push to a live site, no paid skills in a repo, a second review, a check before going live.
